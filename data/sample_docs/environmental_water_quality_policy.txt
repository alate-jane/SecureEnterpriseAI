TASMANIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AND RIPARIAN CATCHMENT POLICY
Classification: Public

1. OVERVIEW
This policy dictates baseline water quality monitoring standards across river catchments and agricultural runoff zones throughout southern and northern Tasmania.

2. TURBIDITY AND SEDIMENTATION THRESHOLDS
Stormwater discharges into Class A aquatic reserves must not exceed 25 NTU (Nephelometric Turbidity Units). Real-time telemetry sensors deployed at designated culverts must report daily median turbidity values to the public environmental monitoring register.

3. REMEDIATION AND STAKEHOLDER CONSULTATION
If dissolved oxygen levels fall below 6.5 mg/L or heavy metal traces are detected above ANZG (2018) trigger levels, operations must cease and local community stakeholders must be notified within 24 hours.

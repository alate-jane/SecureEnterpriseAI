REGIONAL POWER & UTILITIES INFRASTRUCTURE
SUBSTATION MAINTENANCE AND HIGH VOLTAGE SAFETY DIRECTIVE (STANDARD HV-401)
Classification: Internal

1. SCOPE AND MANDATORY PRE-ENTRY REQUIREMENTS
All personnel accessing high-voltage substation zones (including 110kV and 220kV yards) must possess current Level 3 Electrical Safety Induction certification. Work party leaders must conduct an on-site safety briefing and record ambient weather conditions before issuing any Permit to Work (PTW).

2. ARC FLASH PROTECTION AND PPE PROTOCOLS
Switching operations and visual busbar inspections require Minimum Category 4 Arc Flash apparel (minimum rating 40 cal/cm2), full-face shield, and dielectric footwear compliant with AS/NZS 4836. Conductive objects, personal jewellery, and mobile telecommunications equipment must be secured outside the designated exclusion boundary.

3. EMERGENCY ISOLATION AND INCIDENT REPORTING
In the event of an unintended trip, arc flash incident, or equipment telemetry fault, operators must immediately depress the station emergency trip push-button, evacuate to the designated muster point, and contact the Central Network Operations Centre (NOC) within 15 minutes. No re-energisation attempt may occur without written approval from the Senior Electrical Asset Controller.
